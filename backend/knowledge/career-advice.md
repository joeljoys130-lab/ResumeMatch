# Software Engineering Career Development & Growth

## 1. Navigating Career Progression
- Junior Engineer: Focus on execution velocity, clean code standards, and rapid learning from senior engineers.
- Mid-Level Engineer: Focus on end-to-end task ownership, architectural design choices, and cross-functional communication.
- Senior Engineer: Focus on system architecture, mentoring junior developers, reducing technical debt, and driving technical strategy.

## 2. Salary Negotiation & Job Offer Management
- Always research market compensation benchmarks (Level.fyi, Glassdoor) before interviewing.
- Negotiate total compensation package including base salary, performance bonus, and stock options.
